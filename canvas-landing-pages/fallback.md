## About

Have you ever wondered why there are waiters at restaurants? Every time you go to a restaurant, you might want to try a different dish or a completely different cuisine. Now, imagine there are 20 cooking staff in the restaurant's kitchen and no waiters. If you want a specific dish, its your responsibility to find the right chef and ask them to prepare your dish. Now, imagine every customer of the restaurant needs to do that. The place would be a mess with confusion reigning supreme. In Java programs, some times object creation methods also woud end up replicating this same mess if not for factory design patterns and abstract factory design patterns, In this module, you'll learn what factory design patterns are and how to implement them.
